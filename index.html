About Us

XtremeGang is a team made by SaumyaXtreme which focuses on working together, crossing limitations with unity, and making sure no one is left behind. Today you focus, tomorrow you enjoy.

SaumyaXtreme

SaumyaXtreme is the official founder of XtremeGang, and he is also a Python and website developer. The website of XtremeGang was made by him.

Who we are

A crew of players and creators grinding toward the same goal — climbing the ranks, sharpening skills, and backing each other up along the way.

What we do

From tracked points and leaderboards to shared tools and an AI assistant on standby, everything here exists to make progress easier to see and easier to earn.

Why it matters

Solo grinding gets old fast. XtremeGang turns it into something you show up for — a place where effort actually gets recognized.

    Members

      Deleting a member removes their usernames doc, which the Libraries page listens to live — their library box disappears immediately, and they can no longer log in. Their old Firebase Authentication record itself isn't permanently erased, since that needs a privileged Admin SDK key that can't safely live in a browser-side page — but everything visible and every login path is fully cut off.

      Leaderboard Membership

      Adjust Points

      Adding someone to the leaderboard starts them at 0 points. From then on, they'll pick up +2 points automatically for each day that's passed — that catch-up runs the next time they (or you) open the site, since there's no server running in the background to do it on a fixed schedule. "Adjust Points" is for manual awards/penalties on top of that, and gets logged under whichever points type you pick.

      Member Color
